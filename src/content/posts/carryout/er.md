# 隨筆 

Aug 22nd, 2026

今天花了些時間在我的琴上，會琴如會友，即使本人匱乏樂理知識但仍是可以聽出音樂的好與壞。
Spent some time with my instrument today. Meeting the instrument is like meeting a friend; even with my limited knowledge of music theory, I can still discern the quality of music.

於是便即興發揮，雖僅僅只是拉了 E 弦，但我的直覺仍然發揮了我意想不到的作用：
So I improvised. Though I was merely pulling the E string, my intuition still played a role beyond my expectations:

- A_B_C_D A_B_C_d [給我的感覺如此，音樂富於變化，而對比會變得更加迷人]
  *A_B_C_D A_B_C_d [This is how it feels to me: music is rich in change, and contrast makes it even more captivating]*
- 這是那段最有感覺的音頻 [不過本人琴技不佳，略有刺耳，但我想這不能辱沒這段音頻本身的價值]
  *This is the audio clip that felt most right [Though my playing skills are modest and slightly harsh, I don't think it should diminish the intrinsic value of this audio]*
- 經此事件我愈發感到降維與形式化的重要性，如何用我貧瘠的語言去描繪世間規律
  *Through this event, I feel ever more strongly the importance of dimensionality reduction and formalization—how to depict universal laws with my meager language*
- [其實我還意識到畫面對音樂的協調加工的神奇作用]
  *[In fact, I also realized the magical effect of visual imagery in harmonizing and processing music]*
- 就單單是作曲，去記錄這段旋律，我手上都沒有什麼可用到的
  *Even for basic composition—just to record this melody—I have no adequate tools at hand*
- 所以，我認識到我的道...
  *Thus, I have come to realize my path...*
