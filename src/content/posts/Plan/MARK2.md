标签是这个世界上的人常用的工具，而是否符合标签则需要模型对齐，但标签无法概括全部，就像理解音乐不仅仅只有技术分析。

**如何建模？（针对现实中的人、事、物）**
***How to Model? (For people, events, and objects in reality)***

1. **疑惑 → 追问 → 建立标题**
   ***Doubt → Inquiry → Formulate Title***
   * 在研究实验中提出假设是十分重要的，即：该实验要预测什么？建立该实验的目的是什么？
     *Formulating hypotheses in research experiments is crucial: What is this experiment predicting? What is the purpose of establishing this experiment?*
2. **寻找参数 → 逆向工程**
   ***Identify Parameters → Reverse Engineering***
   * 宾州州立学院曾通过大量实验，来检验通过“雷震”揭示地球内部隐藏结构的可行性。在某些层次中，模型建立仅通过逆向解构与重组完成。
     *Penn State once conducted extensive experiments to test the feasibility of revealing Earth's hidden internal structures through "thunderquakes." At certain levels, model construction is accomplished purely through reverse deconstruction and reconstruction.*
   * **“雷震”**：闪电击中地面产生的部分雷声转化为地震波，并在土壤和岩石中传播［类似大气波源］。
     ***"Thunderquake"**: Part of the sound generated when lightning strikes the ground converts into seismic waves, propagating through soil and rock [similar to an atmospheric wave source].*
3. **建立模型 → 践行模型 → 迭代模型 → 完成闭环**
   ***Build Model → Practice Model → Iterate Model → Complete the Closed Loop***
   * 工程思维中，最重要的是优化与迭代，这使得模型更经得起考验。
     *In engineering thinking, optimization and iteration are paramount, enabling models to better withstand tests and scrutiny.*
