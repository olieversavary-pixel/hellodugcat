- Aug 25th, 2026
- 多年时间沉淀，历经回忆，分析，总结三个模块思考  
  *Years of accumulation, through three modules of reflection: recall, analysis, and summary*
  - 我的心流状态  
    *My Flow States*
    - 第一个就是绘画时，当一切变得有趣，会变得兴奋, 而且开心，分享欲变强, 专注到绘画的时刻  
      *1st: Drawing—when everything becomes interesting, I get excited and happy, with a stronger desire to share, fully focused on the moment of drawing.*
    - 第二个就是小提琴,我的舌头会紧绷, 牙关咬紧, 仔细感受身体的那个动作, 和音是否准确  
      *2nd: Playing the violin—my tongue gets tense, my jaw clenches, carefully feeling that physical movement and whether the chord/intonation is accurate.*
    - 第三个是看漫画时,因为特别集中,别人讲话话时我会完全听不在，但是仅仅是有时候,我感性的时候,感兴趣的时候才是这样，很少有这种状况,我只经历过一次  
      *3rd: Reading manga—because I'm so focused, I don't hear people speaking at all. But this only happens sometimes, when I'm emotional or interested. It's rare, and I've only experienced it once.*
    - 第四个就是不断讨论问题的时候,是有时候感觉没讨论几个问题,却已经过去一两个小时,甚至聊了一半天了  
      *4th: Continuously discussing problems with Tools—sometimes it feels like we only discussed a few questions, but one or two hours have passed, or even half a day.*
    - 第五个就是装机箱的时候,因为里面的东西都是根据别人列出来的清单去购买的，所以安装畅通无阻,重要的是去用手去把它们搭建起来，这个时候我侵进心血总计5-6h  
      *5th: Assembling a PC case—since all parts were bought based on a list made by others, the assembly goes smoothly. What matters is building them with my hands; during this time I pour my heart and soul into it for 5–6 hours in total.*
    - 第七个是拆解他人行为与心理动机，对特殊样本的黑箱进行逆向思考，这也让我十分快乐  
      *7th: Deconstructing others' behaviors and psychological motivations, reverse-engineering the black box of unique samples, which also brings me great joy.*
    - 第八个是记录笔记与思考，将结果与思考呈现在纸上或电子设备上归档  
      *8th: Taking notes and reflecting, presenting results and thoughts on paper or electronic devices for archiving.*
    - 第九个读书和写数学推导的时候,感觉也是很专注的,但是可能是带着目的性的专注,所以心流的感觉不是特别强烈，但肯定超过很多平时行的专注状态了  
      *9th: Reading and doing mathematical derivations—I feel very focused, but it might be goal-oriented focus, so the feeling of flow isn't particularly strong, though it certainly exceeds standard daily focus.*
    - 以上是我的心流状态的大致切片，我认为我喜欢反思，追问与破解，喜欢将具体事物概念化并寻找相似结构，喜欢有大量身体反馈的活动，所以我认可多通道整合性的学习方式  
      *The above are rough slices of my flow states. I believe I enjoy reflection, questioning, and deciphering; I like conceptualizing concrete things and finding similar structures, as well as engaging in activities with rich somatic feedback. Therefore, I align with a multimodal integrative learning approach.*
- 今日在返程过程中，听了一首中英文混杂的歌曲，当我听到母语时，并不需要刻意翻译，便可以理解，我认识到第一语言是真正沉于基底核的编码，一这种感觉去衡量第二第三语言学习会有意想不到的好处  
  *Today on my return journey, I listened to a song with mixed Chinese and English lyrics. When I heard my native language, I could understand it naturally without deliberate translation. I realized that one's first language is an encoding truly embedded in the basal ganglia; evaluating second and third language learning through this feeling yields unexpected benefits.*
  - 作为视听能力正常的人，我回忆的功能多以视觉符号挂钩，回想书上内容时，也许真的会捕捉到更多书中的内容，也许盲人的记忆功能更以触觉感知与听觉为主吧...  
    *As a person with normal sight and hearing, my memory recall is mostly linked to visual symbols. When recalling content from a book, I might indeed capture more visual details from it. Perhaps the memory function of visually impaired individuals relies more on tactile perception and hearing...*
