# Thought

Thought is truly fascinating... You ask 'I' why? Of course, because it is invisible; it is elusive. 'I' firmly believes that in the current environment, there does not exist a person (whose mental model is absolutely quantified, whose thinking is completely reversible) — this is also an explanation for the generalization of the cognitive black box.

Because reverse-deconstructing and reassembling-quantifying a person's mental model requires not only a person's thinking prototype, but even more so that person's complete embodied experience. However, we naturally lose the initial data of this dataset...

The characteristics of thought inevitably cause people to build trust and comparable cognitive architectures. I believe this is also the beginning of people's high degree of homogenization and collectivization.

Thought is a compiler and even more a decoder. Different individuals possess different modes of thinking, different cognitive dimensions and directions, and therefore the value of information decoding is different.
