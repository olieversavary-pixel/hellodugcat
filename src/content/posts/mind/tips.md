### Teach a Person to Fish, Not Just Give Them a Fish

One's preferences for the things they love are not fixed; they depend more on accumulated experience, evolving cognition, and shifting behavioral inclinations.

Here are my reflections from today:

But on closer thought, what should you actually be doing? You have already built breadth in painting, violin, programming, and mathematics—so where is the depth?

As for programming, what exactly do you want to learn? Before truly entering a field, in a state of incomplete information, my past choices were mostly drawn to polished project presentations. Intuition is not completely unreliable, but this still needs to be verified.

### Mathematics

Mathematics: merely a tool for dimensionality reduction and formalization.

> If I were blind, perhaps I would love it all the more.

### Violin

Violin: a medium for embodied perception.

New insights emerge while playing: the alternating up-and-down movement of the bow during string changes feels like a program reset.

Usually, the left hand engages first, followed by the right.

Posture is paramount—then intonation—then proficiency; that is how I see it.

### Painting

Painting: merely another form of expression—perhaps a bid to break convention and stand out.

> If I were deaf, perhaps I would love painting all the more.

### Programming

Programming: my best bridge to reality—synthetic creation and system orchestration. I once thought of becoming a hacker, or developing games (though even playing games in real life feels exhausting). I have also considered computer networking, data science, cognitive computing, human-computer interaction, and web development. These, I think, were just illusions that attracted me when I lacked sufficient information.

### Science

Science: I love science and have even wanted to pursue academic research, but I am soberly aware that what I see now is only the sugar-coated surface.

### Human-AI Symbiosis

Human-AI Symbiosis: for now, the logical step is to sample various paths before committing. This requires the quiet accumulation of lived experience.

### In the End

> In the end—like waking from a grand dream, still in the world; whether past or present, we are actors in the play—the Way lies in experience.
